Frequently Asked Questions

Who is allowed to attend these events?

All currently enrolled students, faculty, and staff are welcome. Some specific events may be restricted to first-year students or specific academic departments.

Do I need to register for events separately?

Most cultural and sports events allow walk-ins. Technical workshops usually require prior registration through the student portal because seating is limited. Press Tab to move through this page using only the keyboard.
